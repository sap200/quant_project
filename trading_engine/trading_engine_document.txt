- Building an order book.
An order book is a electronic list of buy and sell orders waiting to be matched. 
It's like 2 sorted list 
 - one for buyer (sorted from highest to lowest - buyer wants best deal)
 - one for seller (soller from lowest price to highest - seller wants the highest price)
 
when highest buy price meets or exceeds the lowest sell price, a trade happen

- Building Position Tracker and profit/Loss

When you buy or sell shares, you need to keep track of your position.
whether you are making money or losing it. 
A position tracks: how many shares you hold.
What you paid on average.
Your profit and Loss 

- Add Risk checks

Before any trade goes through , you need safety checks to prevent loses
Risk check are like guard rails, stop you from buying too much of one stock.
having too much money at risk., or losing too much in single day
