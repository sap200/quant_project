The investment strategy we have built is best described as a **Tactical Machine Learning Overlay** (specifically a *Long-Only Positive Expectation Strategy*). 

In the quant world, this is a sophisticated version of "Market Timing" that uses machine learning to decide when to be "In" (Long) or "Out" (Cash).

### The Investment Strategy in Bullet Points

* **Long-Only Filter:** We only take "Long" positions (buying to sell higher). We never "Short" (betting against) the market.
* **Positive Expectation Entry:** The strategy follows a strict rule: **"Only buy if the model predicts a profit."** If the forecasted return for a stock is $0$ or negative, we treat that stock as "toxic" for that window and ignore it.
* **Tactical Asset Allocation (TAA):** Unlike a "Buy & Hold" strategy that stays in the market forever, this is *Tactical*. It shifts between being 100% invested (Aggressive) and 100% in Cash (Defensive) based on the model's confidence.
* **Cash as a Safe Haven:** When the market looks "bearish" (the model predicts negative returns for all 5 stocks), the strategy moves your entire portfolio into **Cash**. This acts as a shield to prevent major losses during crashes.
* **Equal-Weight Distribution:** To manage risk, we don't put more money into one stock just because the model says it might return more. We split the available cash **equally** among all stocks that passed the "Positive" filter. This prevents one "bad call" from a single stock from ruining the whole portfolio.
* **Horizon-Based Rebalancing:** The strategy rebalances according to your model's prediction window (e.g., every 5 days). It "cleans" the portfolio and resets positions to align with the freshest data available.
